## Installing
1. Download Meteor Client (https://meteorclient.com/download), for help see https://meteorclient.com/faq/installation
2. Download Meteor ServerSeeker using the comically large button above and drop it in your mods folder!

## How to use:
### Searching for servers
<img width="468" height="165" alt="9" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7dac815-501e-4b59-9d5f-d23c87841b80" /><br />
Let's say we want to <b>find cracked servers</b> with at least 2 players online. Because of this I am setting <b>"Cracked" to "Yes" and "Minimum Online Players" to "2"</b>   <br />
The mod searches servers automatically for your current Minecraft version.   <br />
<img width="503" height="375" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa23c664-203f-4235-8440-3f86b3e1aee3" /><br />
After clicking on "Find", we can now just <b>add all servers</b>  <br />
<img width="667" height="176" alt="1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3fdd4a23-ef82-449b-9bd9-d90d0c3dcade" /><br />
And just that easy, the servers are now in your server list!   <br />
<img width="622" height="520" alt="3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/623f1f9c-e0f7-4228-80d0-5a2ca472b4b9" /><br />


### Searching for players
<img width="503" height="375" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa23c664-203f-4235-8440-3f86b3e1aee3" /><br />
Let's say we want to find the player with the name <b>"popiiumaa"</b>. We just type "popiiumaa" into the box, and click on <b>"Find Player"</b>   <br />
<img width="411" height="188" alt="5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c14b0bb7-edef-4aac-b0b9-c9fe15e9c78a" /><br />
Now we can just add the servers we want, or <b>even add all!</b>   <br />
<img width="925" height="466" alt="6" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/31e960cd-45da-4f40-9bc9-d33199785446" /><br />


### Getting players on a server
Simply select a server and click on <b>"Get players"</b>!   <br />
<img width="1000" height="163" alt="7" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/177a3070-4ee6-4ec6-945d-e3b2f0693b50" /><br />
You can now see the player history. Clicking on <b>"Login"</b> will log you into the account in cracked mode.    <br />
<img width="435" height="366" alt="8" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/922fbab9-2a7a-4ce6-843b-813980a1468b" /><br />
